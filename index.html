01 THE REAL STORY & ORIGIN

A Carpenter's Hands. An Architect's Mind.

At 18, my career didn't begin inside an air-conditioned IT cubicle. I was working with wood, nails, and hand tools as a carpenter. Shaping timber taught me something no university syllabus ever could: respect for the raw material, millimeter precision, and the hard truth that you cannot build a lasting structure on a flimsy foundation.

Yet every single day, I felt a relentless hunger to break into tech. I didn't come from a computer science background, and I had no industry mentors. What I had was pure curiosity, endless hours of YouTube tutorials, and late-night persistence. I taught myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WordPress from scratch — failing, rewriting, and pushing forward until the code clicked.

Six years ago, that self-belief unlocked my first commercial breakthrough: joining ERE Business Solutions at Cyberpark, Kozhikode as a WordPress Developer. Later, I honed dynamic frontend skills as a Web Developer at QUADCUBES, mastering fast client turnarounds and modern responsive standards.

My defining leadership chapter was serving for 2.5 years as the Head of the Web Development Department (HOD) at HARIS & CO. I led our web development department, spearheaded high-converting platform architectures for major brands, built custom Shopify & WordPress engines, and managed client delivery pipelines.

Because I had to fight for every piece of knowledge I possess, giving back became my personal mission. I stepped up as the Lead WordPress Mentor at HARIS & CO Academy, and trained students at Techoriz Academy and Skill Mount. To date, I have personally mentored over 2,000+ aspiring developers, helping them launch their own careers in tech.

"Carpentry taught me structure. Code gave me scale. Mentorship gave me purpose. Today, with 80+ delivered websites and 6+ years in the trenches, I treat every client's digital presence like a masterwork built to withstand time."
